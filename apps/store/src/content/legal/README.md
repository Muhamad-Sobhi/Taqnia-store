# Legal Content

- **Purpose:** Store owner-editable legal policy drafts until approved versions are supplied.
- **Audience:** Taqnia content owners and developers.
- **Last updated:** 2026-11-24
- **Related docs:** [Content guidelines](../../../../../docs/design/content-guidelines.md), [Deployment runbook](../../../../../docs/operations/deployment-runbook.md)

Each draft must visibly display `DRAFT - PENDING LEGAL REVIEW` and include a TODO marker. Production launch is blocked until approved policy text replaces every draft.
